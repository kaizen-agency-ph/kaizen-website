Partnerquestions

The usual things agency owners ask before handing over a client.

Will my clients ever know you exist?

Not unless you tell them. We don't put our name on deliverables, we don't contact your clients directly, and we're happy to sign your NDA. If you'd like us on a call, we join as part of your team.

Who owns the work?

You and your client do. Everything is built in your or your client's accounts, and all files, logins and source are handed over. Nothing is locked to us.

How do you handle client logins and access?

We prefer delegated access, such as Business Manager partner access, Google account roles and CMS user accounts, over shared passwords. Access is removed when a project ends, if you'd like.

How do we communicate day to day?

However your team already does: Slack, ClickUp, Asana, Trello or email. You get one point of contact and updates written so you can forward them to your client as is.

What do you need to get started?

A short call, your brief or the client's goals, brand assets and access to the relevant accounts. We send back a scope and quote, and once you approve it, we start.

Can we start small?

Yes, and we'd recommend it. Send one project or one client first. If it works, we talk about a retainer.
